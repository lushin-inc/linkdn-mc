Talk Tracks

1)First-Call Up-Front Contract

“All right, I tell you what, let me set the stage for us real quick. The point of this call is pretty casual. I know you already talked with the call center. I’m gonna give you some options and help you understand what this is, what it costs, and anything else you want to know. At the end, if it seems like a good fit and it’s what you’re looking for, I’ll typically pause and schedule a separate time to walk you through some options. If it’s not what you’re looking for, no hard feelings. We can part ways as friends. Does that work?”
Opening Pain Question
“I’ve got the notes from the call center, and I’ll be your final point of contact. There might be a little bit of redundancy, but just so I’m not reading you a brochure about Home Nation….. help me understand what piqued your interest in having this call. Then I’ll try to help any way I can.”

2)Transition to the Options Call

“All right, so at this point, every question you could ask me is probably some variation of, ‘What do you guys actually have?’ Which is correct. At this point, I’ll typically pause and schedule a separate time to walk you through some options, if you’d like…...” (mute)

3)Scheduling the Go/No-Go Call

“Okay, I tell you what, I’m going to send you everything I showed you so you can talk this over….. I don’t want to call and interrupt your day at any point…...Do you mind if we set a five-minute placeholder for [whatever day] at [whatever time], I'll follow your lead on what time works best….. And the point of that is….... If you wanna get set up, I can help with that. If you decide it isn’t for you, just send me an email or text and we’ll cancel the call. Or if you have questions, I can answer those too.”
