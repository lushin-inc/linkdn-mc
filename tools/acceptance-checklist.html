50%+ Acceptance Rate Checklist

0 of 8
  • Send between 8 AM and 11 AM their time, Monday to Thursday
  • Split your LinkedIn searches by geography if you need to
  • Outside that window, requests get buried
  • Check their recent activity — when did they last post or comment?
  • The more recent, the better
  • Tick “Posted on LinkedIn” in a Sales Nav search to filter out inactive profiles
  • Look for investment in their profile picture, banner and tagline
  • About, featured, experiences and skills filled out
  • A real connection count
  • 500+ connections
  • Profile picture that builds trust
  • Banner and tagline calling out your ICP
  • Two client recommendations
  • Experiences, about and featured speaking directly to your prospects
  • At least two posts a week
  • If you're not active, prospects read your request as automated or fake
  • More mutuals means more familiarity, which means more accepts
  • Not a huge lever on its own — but build your connections inside your niche
  • Only send requests to people in your target market
  • If you're under 40–50% acceptance, go manual
  • Manual runs 10–15%+ higher than automated, because you vet every prospect
  • Aim for 40 requests a day, Monday to Friday, in 10–15 minutes
